## Resolve contradictions D2, D3, D4, D9 and D12 in spec version 1

- labels: type: docs, area: spec, phase: 0-foundations
- milestone: Phase 0: Foundations
- state: closed
- parent: Phase 0 tracking
- fields: Status=Done; Phase=Phase 0; Area=spec; Priority=P1; Size=M; Gate=A

### Problem

The research documents contradict each other in places (listed as D1 to D12 on the [PDR](https://github.com/Karthik-beta/fiscalclock/wiki/PDR)). The spec must not silently pick a side.

### Outcome

Spec version 1 resolves these explicitly: `year_number: "table"` is reserved for 0.2 (D2); the anchor weekday derives from `week.starts_on` (D3, [ADR-0028](https://github.com/Karthik-beta/fiscalclock/wiki/ADR-0028-anchor-weekday-from-week-start)); a week pattern inside a fixed-date year is rejected until `stub` is decided (D4); the output flag is `has_extra_week` (D9); `anchored` subdivision is reserved for 0.2 (D12). D1 (`stub`) stays open in [ADR-0024](https://github.com/Karthik-beta/fiscalclock/wiki/ADR-0024-stub-semantics).
