New Features New FiscalYear.current() and FiscalQuarter.current() class constructors (#4) Test dependency on pytest-mock has been added (#4) New setup_fiscal_calendar function to change start date of fiscal calendar (#5) Python 3.7 and 3.8 are now tested, although they always worked The benefit of setup_fiscal_calendar is that it validates your input. Direct modification of fiscalyear global variables is now discouraged and support may be dropped in a future release.
